requirements-lock.txt records the exact Python build-environment versions.
docs/THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md lists their verified PyPI metadata declarations and
source archives, including GPL/LGPL components. Unknown license metadata is
explicitly marked and must be checked against the upstream source archive.
The list covers the locked environment; it does not assert that every package
is included in every executable. The build copies installed package notices
into the application's Contents/Resources/Licenses directory.
